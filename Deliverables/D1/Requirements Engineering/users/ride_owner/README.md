# Ride Owner — Requirements

**Status:** draft complete, under team review · **Updated:** 2026-09-24

The Ride Owner is a verified IIT Kanpur user who initiates a ride to a shared destination and acts as the manager for the pool.

**Key differences from Rider:**
- The Ride Owner creates the ride with a fixed vehicle capacity upfront.
- Riders are added automatically upon joining (no manual accept/reject by the Owner).
- The Ride Owner does not currently have AI agent features.
- The Ride Owner coordinates with the driver entirely outside of the application.
- The Ride Owner can mark no-shows during pickup, which triggers automatic fare recalculation by the app.
