\section{Gaps and lessons}\label{sec:gaps}

This section records where the project fell short of its own process. The final Jira cleanup on 5 November
set statuses and assignees only; the estimates and sprints of past issues were left as they were.

\subsection{Jira}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Estimates.} \JiraWithEstimate{} of \JiraIssues{} issues have a story point estimate, so the
    team planned and tracked sprints by issue count rather than by estimated effort.
  \item \textbf{Sprints and fields.} \JiraNoSprint{} issues are in no sprint, \JiraNoLabels{} have no labels
    and \JiraUnassigned{} have no assignee.
  \item \textbf{Statuses lagged behind the work.} \JiraResolvedFinalDay{} issues were marked resolved on
    5 November, many of them for work merged earlier, so resolution dates are not a reliable record of when
    work finished.
  \item \textbf{Issues recorded late.} \JiraCreatedFinalDay{} issues were created on 5 November. They include
    \JiraDefectBugsFinalDay{} of the \JiraDefectBugs{} defect Bugs, which were recorded after the defects were
    fixed rather than when they were found.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Code review}
\PullsNoReview{} of the \PullsMerged{} merged pull requests have no recorded review from another member,
although the handout expects a review on significant pull requests. Nothing enforced it, because
\texttt{master} has no branch protection.

\subsection{Schedule and scope}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Late start on testing.} \texttt{master} has no commits for \CommitGapDays{} days between
    \CommitGapFrom{} and \CommitGapTo{}, and Deliverable 3 testing started only at the end of that gap.
  \item \textbf{Late deployment.} The platform was chosen and deployed on 5 November, the day before the
    deadline, which left little time to run the system under real conditions.
  \item \textbf{Performance targets.} The p95 and p99 latency targets were not met, and the performance report
    states what would need rerunning before making a stronger claim.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Lessons}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Test against the real database early.} The unit suite cleared its 80\,\% coverage gate with
    the database mocked, yet from 14 to 28 October every seat request failed with a 500 on a real database
    (\pr{84}), and no test showed it. The coverage gate is necessary but does not show that the parts work
    together.
  \item \textbf{Update Jira as the work happens.} A status or Bug recorded at the end costs as much time as one
    recorded on the day and is worth less as evidence.
  \item \textbf{Turn the process into a rule.} Requiring a review through branch protection from the first day
    would have settled the review gap without relying on anyone remembering.
  \item \textbf{Deploy early.} A walking skeleton on the final platform during Deliverable 2 would have tested
    the deployment choice while there was still time to change it.
\end{itemize}
